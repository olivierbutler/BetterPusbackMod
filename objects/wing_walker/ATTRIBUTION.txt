Changes made for BetterPushback:
- Removed the hammer prop and discarded the original hammering motion.
- Re-posed the rig for held STOP, STANDBY, and final-clear signals.
- Added orange marshalling wands and a green final-clear wand/light.
- Baked the rigged character into three static, dataref-selected OBJ8 poses.
- Repacked the diffuse texture into an X-Plane texture atlas and added a
  matching lit texture for the green final-clear wand.
- Rotated the character to face the aircraft when instantiated by the plugin.
- Airport employee revision: deleted the hard hat and brim, created a new
  bare scalp with short hair, and added corded foam earplugs.
- Replaced the construction uniform's appearance with a lime safety vest,
  silver strips, ID badge, navy short-sleeved shirt and navy trousers.
- Replaced the exposed arms and hands with new skinned arm/wrist meshes and
  separate palms, four curled fingers and thumbs gripping black wand handles.
- Set the standby wands to 45 degrees downward, separated the crossing stop
  arms in depth, and added orange wand illumination to the lit atlas.
- Indexed shared vertex records to reduce the runtime OBJ file size.
- Added night illumination for the front/rear vest fabric and silver strips;
  the raised clear wand remains green in both the OBJ and editable pose keys.
